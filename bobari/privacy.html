"Knitting Chart Designer" (the App) is provided by an individual developer.

Data collection

The App requires no account and does not collect or transmit any personal information. Your charts and settings are stored only on your device. The App works fully offline and has no capability to send data anywhere.

Exported images, PDFs and backup files are created only when you share them yourself. The App never uploads anything automatically. Please handle shared files with care.

Ads

The App contains no ads and no ad SDKs.

The one-time Pro purchase is processed by Google Play / App Store billing. The developer never receives your payment details.
