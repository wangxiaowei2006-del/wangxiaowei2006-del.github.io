Español

English

  1. Your study data never leaves the device. Progress, review queue, streak and language choice are stored only on your phone. No account, no sign-up, no servers.
  2. Ads (free version). We use Google AdMob to show interstitial ads between practice sessions. AdMob may collect: advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions, for ad serving and fraud prevention. See the Google Privacy Policy. After purchasing "Remove Ads", the app no longer requests ads.
  3. Purchases. The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase ($4.99) is processed by Google Play Billing; the developer never sees any payment data.
  4. Voice. Speech is synthesized on-device (system TTS); no audio or text is uploaded.
  5. The developer collects no personal information. Apart from the AdMob SDK described above, the app sends no data to anyone.
  6. Children. The app targets professional drivers (18+); it is not directed at children.
  7. Deleting data: uninstalling the app deletes all local data. The advertising ID is managed in your device settings.
  8. Changes: updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
